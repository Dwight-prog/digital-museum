Curator's Note

About the Digital Museum

Purpose

To present the Philippines' national symbols and examine their importance to Filipino identity, history, and culture. The museum aims to give visitors a deeper understanding of the stories, meanings, and values represented by these symbols.

Goal

To promote the appreciation and awareness of the Philippines' heritage by helping visitors understand the significance of national symbols and how they showcase the history, traditions, values, and identity of Filipinos.

This digital museum is an online educational exhibit that explores the national symbols of the Philippines and their connection to the history, culture, and identity of Filipinos.

Visitors can explore various exhibits that showcase important national symbols, learn about their history and significance, and understand the values they represent. Through an interactive museum experience, the website encourages visitors to appreciate how these symbols continue to represent Filipinos and their shared heritage.
